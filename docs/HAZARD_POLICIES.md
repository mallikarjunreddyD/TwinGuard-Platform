# Hazard policies

configs/fusion/hazards.yaml defines the default scenario-monitoring policy. It declares accepted asset types, installations/instrument ranges and calibration status, scoped green requirements, hazard rules, freshness/coincidence limits, physical compatibility, independent-source requirements, actions, persistence and recovery. FusionPolicy validates unique IDs, asset/type references and feasible distinct-modality counts. All qualified findings require good valid calibrated evidence; this common minimum cannot be relaxed by an unvalidated policy field.

The example critical pressure limit is 1,150,000 Pa: the fixed synthetic model's nominal full-load pressure envelope reaches 1,100,000 Pa and the example adds a 50,000 Pa reserve. The critical temperature example is 240 degC: nominal full-load temperature reaches 230 degC with a 10 degC reserve. These are illustrative research parameters, not equipment/site/statutory limits or approvals. An early development smoke used an inappropriate 180 degC example at the nominal reference; its false reds were preserved and corrected using the fixed nominal envelope before fresh final seeds. See evidence fusion/development_policy_review.json.

Critical raw pressure/temperature crossings use their own rule path, 10 s freshness, known calibrated instrument, valid range/units, good quality and adequate clocks. They may raise red immediately with one credible source even when LiDAR is stale/missing and the operating model is out of range. Operating residuals instead raise amber. Policy actions produce operator-facing advisories and review records.

Localized geometry produces amber, requires qualified registration/calibration/coverage and persists until recorded repair or explicit review. Thermal-only evidence produces an amber inspection-context advisory when image-only. Two independent recent geometry/thermal findings require compatible published physical regions for the stronger red combination; image-only cannot satisfy it. Geometry/process-temperature combination requires compatible asset context and timing and remains amber. Shared sources/calibration never count twice.

BW-01 green coverage requires current pressure/temperature limit evidence, pressure operating-reference coverage and a supported localized inspection. A positive required channel is covered abnormal evidence, not mislabeled missing; it cannot yield green while activation/persistence is pending. Global/support evidence is not implied by local coverage. Other assets without normal-coverage requirements remain unknown by F1, even if no abnormality is reported. Configuring green for more assets requires explicit monitoring requirements.

configs/fusion/site-template.yaml is conservative and intentionally unconfigured for critical limits/calibration. Deployment configuration supplies engineering limits, sensor installation/calibration references, clock validation and operator procedures. The engine evaluates the verified producer metadata under those policies.
